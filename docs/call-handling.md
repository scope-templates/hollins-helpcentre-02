# Call handling

What everybody on a phone at Hollins Rota is supposed to do. Short on purpose.

1. Say at the start that the call is recorded for training and quality. Every call, every time, before
   anything else is discussed.
2. Never read a card number, expiry or security code aloud, and never type one into a ticket or a chat.
   Send the secure link; the customer enters the card themselves.
3. When a customer signs up on a call, tell them they have fourteen days to cancel for a full refund, and
   put it in the confirmation email.
4. Do not promise a feature, a date or a price we have not published. "It is on the roadmap and I cannot
   give you a date" is the whole answer.
5. If you say you will do something, write it on the account the same day.
